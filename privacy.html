Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What this app is

Hermes Assistant is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant operated by a single individual (the "owner") exclusively for the owner's own use. There are no other users, no accounts, and no public service.

Google account data

The app accesses the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) using OAuth 2.0 with the owner's explicit consent. This access is used solely for the owner's personal tasks, such as compiling a daily newsletter digest, drafting replies on the owner's behalf, and managing the owner's own files. No Google account data of any other person is requested, accessed, or stored by the app.

Where data lives

Email content collected for digests is processed and stored locally on the owner's own machines. Summaries or context may be sent to the large-language-model providers the owner has configured in order to generate responses. The owner is responsible for those providers' handling of data.

No tracking

This website sets no cookies, contains no analytics, no trackers, and no scripts of any kind. It serves static pages only.

No sharing or selling

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for any purpose.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates all issued tokens.
