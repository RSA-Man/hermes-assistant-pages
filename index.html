Hermes Assistant

A personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own hardware.

Hermes Assistant is a private, self-hosted assistant used by a single individual to manage their own email digests, calendar, documents and daily tasks. It is not a public service, it has no user accounts, and it does not collect data about visitors.

This site exists to satisfy the application-homepage requirement for the assistant's Google OAuth integration.
